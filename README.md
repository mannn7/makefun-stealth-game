**Moonlight Heist** — a top-down stealth game for make.fun's "Make the best stealth game" bounty ($50 in USDC).

You are Wren, a cat burglar slipping through the Halden Gallery after dark. Steal the Moon Opal from its pedestal, then reach the green EXIT mat — without ever being seen. Five guards patrol the halls with sweeping vision cones; display cases, crates and pillars block their sight, and the dark corners of the floor hide you. Run and your footsteps give you away. Get spotted and the alarm rises: guards investigate, then chase. Get caught and the night is over.

## How to play

- **WASD / arrow keys** — move (walk quietly by default)
- **Shift** — run (fast, but loud: guards hear your footsteps)
- **C or Ctrl** — sneak (slow, much harder to spot, almost silent)
- **M** — mute sound
- **Click** the on-screen buttons to start, retry and play again

Steal the gem (walk over it), then reach the exit. Finish unseen for the **Ghost** rating.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. No build step, no network — the game is plain static files under `game/`.
